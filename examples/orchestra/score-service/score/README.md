Why not the Symphony No. 40 (K. 550) the plan named: Mutopia has no K. 550, and the only free file on IMSLP for its first movement is a piano transcription under CC BY-NC-SA, not a full orchestra. The operator chose No. 25 instead (29 Oct 2026): the same key, the opening of the film *Amadeus*, public domain.

The file's tracks, as read by `score.py`: `one` oboes, `two` and `three` horns, `eight` violins I, `nine` violins II, `ten` violas, `eleven` cellos and basses on one staff, as Mozart wrote them; the basses play it an octave lower, as a double bass sounds. 414 bars with the exposition's repeat, 4/4, 152 to the quarter. The file's own volume and program changes are not used.
